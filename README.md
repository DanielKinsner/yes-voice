# yes-voice

Research notes for an offline voice restoration and vocal polish product.

## Contents

- `docs/research/consolidated-voice-restoration-research.md` - deduplicated working synthesis of the research findings.
- `docs/research/voice restoration research.md` - product-oriented research on offline vocal polish architecture, model choices, deployment tradeoffs, and licensing.
- `docs/research/Offline Voice Restoration Tech Stack.md` - deeper technical notes on DSP, local ML inference, and standalone app architecture foundations.
- `docs/research/3rd deep research voice restoration.md` - additional deep research on product modules, competitive landscape, Rust/Tauri implementation, and licensing caveats.
- `docs/research/openai voice restoration deep research2.md` - additional OpenAI research on restoration-workstation product shape, frontier models, C++/JUCE options, and DAW/ARA strategy.

## Status

This repository currently contains research and architecture notes. Implementation code has not been added yet.
